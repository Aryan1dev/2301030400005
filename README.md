🎯 Overview
This repository serves as the central documentation for a suite of technology platforms developed in and focused on the Indian market, particularly Ahmedabad. Each project is designed to solve critical local challenges using modern technology, AI, and hyper-local context.

The three primary applications in this portfolio are:

CareLink: A 24/7 Telehealth and Prescription Savings platform.

IndiaOn: An AI-driven, gamified E-Learning platform for competitive exams and professional skills.

Apex Goods: An E-Commerce platform using context-aware AI for highly personalized shopping.

1. 🏥 CareLink: Complete Healthcare at Your Fingertips
Mission
To make quality healthcare accessible, affordable, and instant for patients in Ahmedabad and across India through telehealth and prescription savings.

Key Features
24/7 On-Demand Telehealth: Connect instantly with licensed doctors anywhere in India.

AI-Powered Symptom Checker: Guides users on appropriate care decisions.

Prescription Savings: Compare pharmacy prices and save up to 80% on medications using a free CareLink coupon.

Local Doctor Booking: Find and book appointments with 2,000+ Verified Doctors in Ahmedabad.

Key Metrics: Serving 50,000+ Happy Patients.


2. 📚 IndiaOn: Learn Smarter, Achieve More (EdTech)
Mission
To democratize and enhance education in India by combining the best global EdTech features (Coursera, Khan Academy, Duolingo, edX, Quizlet) into one powerful, localized platform.

Key Features
Gamified Mastery: Uses Duolingo-style points, streaks, and leaderboards combined with Khan Academy's mastery system for engaging, effective learning.

Comprehensive Catalog: 200+ Competitive Exam Prep courses (JEE, NEET, UPSC) and 500+ Tech/Professional courses (AI/ML, Data Science).

University Partnerships: Courses verified by IITs, IIMs, Harvard, and MIT.

Multilingual Support: Content available in Hindi & English.

Impact: Over 3.2 Million students enrolled in Competitive Exam Prep alone.


3. 🛍️ Apex Goods: AI-Powered E-Commerce
Mission
To elevate lifestyles across Ahmedabad by providing a revolutionary, highly personalized shopping experience that anticipates the user's needs based on real-world context.

Key Features
Context-Aware Recommendations (The "Soo Smart" Factor):

Weather & Location: Recommends products based on real-time local conditions (e.g., umbrellas during monsoon).

Time-of-Day: Personalizes suggestions for morning, afternoon, or evening routines.

Local Event Awareness: Promotes festival-specific collections (e.g., Navratri Chaniya Cholis) with guaranteed fast local delivery.

"Viral in Ahmedabad" Trends: Community-driven insights on what neighbors are buying.
